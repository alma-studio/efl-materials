The rule

write it, say it

We write

  • 1 August
  • 3 March 2026
  • 21 December

We say

  • the first of August
  • the third of March, twenty twenty-six
  • the twenty-first of December

Two things to remember:

1. We say the before the number and of before the month.

2. We use on with a date: My birthday is on the fifth of June.

Years

how we say them

Two numbers

  • 1985 → nineteen eighty-five
  • 2026 → twenty twenty-six
  • 2019 → twenty nineteen

Careful with 2000–2009

  • 2000 → two thousand
  • 2005 → two thousand and five
  • 2010 → twenty ten
